## 4. Hafta-1 Danışmanlık İçerikleri (2 karusel — onaya hazır taslak)

### Karusel A: "What does a hair transplant in Türkiye really cost? (2026)"
1. HOOK: "€1,500–€3,500. But what's actually inside that price? 👇"
2. "Indicative 2026 range: €1,500–€3,500 in Türkiye vs £8,000–£15,000 in the UK.*"
3. "Usually included: procedure, medication, aftercare kit, first follow-up."
4. "Often included: hotel + airport transfers. Always confirm in writing."
5. "Not included: your flights — and sometimes extra grafts. Ask before paying."
6. "Rule #1: a real quote is **written and itemised**. Verbal packages are a red flag."
7. CTA: "Full 2026 cost guide → link in bio (Guides). Questions? DM or WhatsApp — free consultation."
*Slayt 2 dipnotu: "Indicative ranges — final price depends on your case and the clinic's written quote."

### Karusel B: "5 checks before you book ANY clinic in Türkiye"
1. HOOK: "Istanbul has hundreds of clinics. These 5 checks separate the serious from the risky."
2. "1️⃣ Who actually performs the procedure — surgeon or technicians?"
3. "2️⃣ Is the doctor licensed and the facility authorised? Ask for documents."
4. "3️⃣ Written, itemised treatment plan — or walk away."
5. "4️⃣ What does the guarantee cover — and what voids it?"
6. "5️⃣ Aftercare: named contact, remote follow-up process."
7. CTA: "We run these checks for you — free first consultation. Link in bio · WhatsApp."
